\section*{I. Basic Information}

Personal homepage: \href{https://upc20zcheng.github.io/}{https://upc20zcheng.github.io/}

Basic information: Zhen Cheng, male, Han ethnicity, from Lixin, Anhui Province, is a lecturer and master's supervisor. He graduated from China University of Petroleum (East China) with a Ph.D. in Management Science and Engineering.

Research directions: green intelligent accounting and finance; credit enhancement through accounting data standards; computing-power-electricity coordination and new forms of smart economy.

Projects led and participated in: He has led one Anhui Provincial Social Science Fund Youth Project, two department- and municipal-level projects, and one key project of the Fuyang Normal University Youth Talent Fund. He has participated in four national, provincial, and Ministry of Education projects, as well as one industry-sponsored project.

Publications: In the past five years, as first author or corresponding author, he has published more than ten academic papers in domestic and international academic journals and outlets including \textit{Energy}, \textit{Journal of Cleaner Production}, \textit{China Population, Resources and Environment}, \textit{Industrial Economics Research}, \textit{Commercial Research}, \textit{Inquiry into Economic Issues}, \textit{Jianghuai Times}, and \textit{Technoeconomics \& Management Research}. These outlets include CAS Q1 journals, Category B journals recognized by the National Natural Science Foundation of China, FMS high-quality Chinese journals, and CSSCI journals.

Academic service: Reviewer for \textit{International Review of Economics \& Finance}, \textit{International Review of Financial Analysis}, \textit{Environmental Engineering and Management Journal}, \textit{Frontiers in Public Health}, \textit{PLOS ONE}, and other journals.

Courses taught: \textit{Management Quantitative Analysis: Methods and Techniques} and \textit{Managerial Accounting} for undergraduates; \textit{Statistical Analysis Methods} and \textit{Theory and Practice of Financial Management} for graduate students.
