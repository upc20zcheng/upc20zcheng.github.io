Profile

About

Zhen Cheng is a lecturer from Lixin, Anhui Province. He received his Ph.D. in Management Science and Engineering from China University of Petroleum (East China). His work combines quantitative analysis with policy relevance to study how accounting standards, environmental institutions, and digital transformation influence firm behavior and regional development.

He has served as principal investigator for the Anhui Provincial Social Science Planning Youth Project, the Anhui Provincial Finance Research Project, and the Fuyang Social Science Planning Project, while also participating in multiple national, provincial, and industry-sponsored projects.

Intelligent Finance
