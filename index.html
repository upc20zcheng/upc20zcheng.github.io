Profile

About

Zhen Cheng is a lecturer from Lixin, Anhui Province. He earned his Ph.D. in Management Science and Engineering from China University of Petroleum (East China). His recent work examines how institutional design, environmental regulation, and digital finance reshape firm behavior, carbon outcomes, and regional development.

Across teaching, research, and review service, his work bridges quantitative analysis with policy relevance. He has led projects supported by the Anhui Provincial Social Science Planning Fund, the Anhui Provincial Finance Research Program, and the Fuyang Social Science Planning Project.

Research

Current Directions

Accounting Data Standards

Investigating mechanisms that improve the credibility, comparability, and governance value of accounting information in evolving institutional settings.

Intelligent Finance

Studying digital transformation, financial intelligence, and the managerial consequences of platformized and data-driven decision systems.

Carbon Transition

Analyzing the firm- and city-level effects of environmental regulation, low-carbon policy, and green finance on emissions and productivity.

Smart Economy

Exploring new forms of economic coordination shaped by digital infrastructure, ecological constraints, and innovation ecosystems.
