01Profile

Profile
Zhen Cheng holds a Ph.D. in Management from China University of Petroleum (East China) and is a lecturer and master's supervisor.
Research interests
Green intelligent accounting and finance; Credit enhancement through accounting data standards; Environmental institutions and corporate finance; Computing-power–electricity coordination and new forms of smart economy
Projects led and participated in
Principal investigator for one Anhui Provincial Social Science Fund Youth Project, two department- and municipal-level projects, and one key project of the Fuyang Normal University Youth Talent Fund. Participant in four national, provincial, and Ministry of Education projects, as well as one industry-sponsored project.
Publications
In the past five years, as first author or corresponding author, he has published more than ten academic papers in domestic and international journals and outlets including International Review of Economics & Finance, Energy, Journal of Cleaner Production, Reform and Strategy, China Population, Resources and Environment, Industrial Economics Research, Commercial Research, Inquiry into Economic Issues, Jianghuai Times, and Technoeconomics & Management Research.
Academic service
Reviewer for International Review of Economics & Finance, International Review of Financial Analysis, Environmental Engineering and Management Journal, Frontiers in Public Health, Frontiers in Environmental Science, PLOS ONE, Frontiers in Sustainable Food Systems, and other journals.
Graduate recruitment
Academic Master of Business Administration students in accounting, enterprise management, and technological economics and management; Master of Professional Accounting (MPAcc) students.
Courses taught
Management Quantitative Analysis: Methods and Techniques and Managerial Accounting for undergraduates; Statistical Analysis Methods and Theory and Practice of Financial Management for graduate students.
